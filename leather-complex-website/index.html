How a hide becomes leather here

Four stages, one site — from the first sort to the loading dock.

01

Intake & sorting

Raw hides arrive, are sorted by grade and size, and prepared for soaking.

02

Tanning

Hides turn through liming, pickling, and tanning drums until they reach wet blue or crust.

03

Finishing

Sides are shaved, dyed, and finished to the thickness and texture a buyer specifies.

04

Dispatch

Finished leather is graded, measured, packed, and sent out to buyers.

Built for volume, run by hand.

Inside the complex, raw hides are soaked and turned through rotating drums before moving to the finishing floor. Every stage, from the first wash to the final roll, is checked by hand, so what leaves the gate meets the grade a buyer asked for.

Located at Plot 422, Zone 5, West Bengal.

Wet blue & crust processing

Hides move through liming, deliming, pickling, and tanning drums to reach wet blue or crust stage, ready for finishing or for sale in semi-processed form.

Finishing & grading

Crust leather is shaved, retanned, dyed, and finished to the thickness, grain, and texture a buyer specifies, then graded by hand.

Bulk packing & dispatch

Finished sides are rolled, measured, and packed for dispatch to garment, footwear, and upholstery makers.
